Scientific disclaimer. This is a simulation. No human subjects, no real-world enforcement, no policy implication. No parameter is calibrated to empirical data. Every number here is a property of a set of equations that were chosen, not measured. Results are statements about model structure — never claims about real institutions or real people.

01.1 The complaint

Two CCE metrics would not respond to anything: abuse detection delay and the governance quality index. Across every society, every seed and every parameter, both traces looked like static around a fixed level. That reading was correct, and it was not a tuning problem. Both metrics were structurally severed from the treatment variable.

Governance quality — white noise by construction

gov.quality = 0.5
            + 0.2 * mean(competence)
            + 0.2 * mean(ethics)
            - 0.6 * corrupt_frac

competence and ethics are drawn as r·z + √(1−r²)·N(0,1) with r = 0.20 and r = 0.00. The mean of ~64 such draws is distributed N(0, 1/√n) — so G is white noise centred on 0.5 with SD ≈ 0.03. That is precisely the observed 0.389–0.630 band.

The allocation rule never appears in the expression. Worse, nothing carries between years: G is redrawn from scratch annually. An index with no memory cannot have a trend, a regime, or a response.

Detection delay — floored before it starts

p_detect = 1 − (1−eff)^checks_per_year
eff      = eff × (0.85 + 0.3 × gov_quality)
# plus: forced detection at max_undetected_duration

Under the default regime p_detect sits close to 1, and the hard duration cap force-detects whatever survives. Nearly every situation is caught in the year it begins, so the metric measures the sampling noise on a near-zero mean — the observed 0.02–0.08 range.

The one governance coupling that exists is 0.85 + 0.3·G. Since G ≈ 0.5 always, that multiplier is ≈ 1.0 forever. It is a wire that carries no signal.

01.2 What this instrument changes

SENTINEL keeps the same two outcome variables and rebuilds everything upstream of them. Five structural changes, each of which converts a dead parameter into a live one:

MechanismWhat it doesWhat it makes visible
Institutional inertia Gt = 0.84·Gt−1 + 0.16·G* — G becomes a slow state carrying its own history. Trends, regimes and recovery times instead of an annual coin flip.
Two-way coupling G multiplies detection capability through 0.30 + 1.35·G, and detected capture clears seats, feeding back into G. Governance and safeguarding stop being independent panels on the same dashboard.
Red Queen concealment Adversaries track detection pressure and invest in concealment up to a ceiling set by λ. Why buying raw detection capability shows diminishing, partly self-cancelling returns.
Channel correlation Effective channels = 1 + (k−1)(1−ρ). At ρ = 0.95, eight oversight bodies behave as one. Why adding oversight to a system with one point of failure changes nothing.
Capture cascade Captured officials recruit each other and suppress the audit function past a threshold. A genuine bifurcation: G is bimodal, not noisy. Two basins, one boundary.

01.3 The honesty device

Three parameters are wired into the sensitivity sampler and into nothing else: CCE's own leader_competence_iq_link, an allocation-rule weight standing in for the A/B/C treatment, and a population scale factor. They are sampled across their full range every run and read by no line of the kernel.

Their measured influence is the screen's noise floor. If any of them ranks alongside a real lever, the screen is broken and everything on the Sensitivity tab should be discarded. This is the check that makes the rest of the rankings worth reading — and it is the check CCE never had, which is how two null metrics survived to be plotted for 500 years.

01.4 The three oversight regimes

These replace CCE's A/B/C allocation arms, which had no causal path to either metric. Every regime faces an identical external shock history for a given seed — the same austerity episodes and interest surges, in the same years, at the same severities.

Then open Tipping and find the boundary between the two governance basins. Then run Sensitivity to see the full ranking, with the decoys sitting at the noise floor where they belong.

03.3 Reading the ranking

04.1 The two governance basins

The capture cascade is a positive feedback with two stable fixed points. Below a boundary in the (independence × capture pressure) plane, audits clear captured officials faster than capture spreads, and the system settles in a clean basin. Above it, captured officials shield each other, audit effectiveness folds, and capture becomes self-sustaining. There is no gentle middle: the transition from 0% to 100% collapse happens over a very narrow band.

This is the qualitative behaviour CCE could not produce. Its governance index was a fresh iid draw each year, so it had exactly one basin and no memory with which to stay in it.

05.1 Kernel

One implementation, running live in this page. Population is a stochastic age-structured compartment model of hidden situations across 100,000 citizens; the 64 offices are simulated individually, because that is where the interesting dynamics live. Determinism is exact under seed — the same seed and parameters replay identically.

Detection

k_eff    = 1 + (k − 1)(1 − ρ)                      effective independent channels
checks   = 1 / interval
g_gate   = 0.30 + 1.35·G                          governance as a real multiplier
vis(a)   = 1 − exp(−a / 7)                        traces accumulate with duration
q(a)     = yield · g_gate · (1 − conceal) · (1 + 0.65·vis(a))
w_eff    = reporter · (0.25 + 0.75·G)             protection you can't rely on isn't protection
q_rep    = (0.08 + 0.42·reporter) · g_gate · (1 − conceal)
p_det(a) = 1 − (1 − q(a))^(k_eff·checks) · (1 − q_rep)^(w_eff·checks)
p_det(a) = 1                                      if a ≥ hard duration cap

Red Queen concealment

pressure   = p_det evaluated at age 0
conceal   += 0.30 · (0.94 · λ · pressure − conceal)

λ sets the ceiling of adaptation, not merely the rate. An earlier build had λ control only the speed of convergence — every λ > 0 then reached the same equilibrium and the lever measured as null in the screen. That is the same failure mode as CCE's governance index, caught by the screen rather than by inspection, which is the argument for having the screen.

Onset

rate  = 0.0030 · (1 − 0.42 · pressure) · (1 + 0.35 · shock)
onset ~ Binomial(N − burden, rate)

Detection pressure carries a general deterrent effect, so oversight reduces the incidence of harm as well as its duration. Both channels are visible on the mechanism trace.

Capture and audit

shield  = 1 / (1 + exp((cap_frac − 0.30) / 0.055))       collusive suppression
a_eff   = ι · (0.22 + 0.78·budget) · (0.03 + 0.97·shield)
hazard  = 0.022 · κ · exp(−1.25·ethics)
             · (1 + 1.8 · min(tenure/term, 2.5))          tenure accumulates exposure
             · (1 + 4.5 · cap_frac)                       capture is contagious
             · (1 − 0.82 · a_eff)                         deterrence
clearing: audit detects a captured official with prob a_eff; rotation at term clears unconditionally

The contagion term is what makes this a saddle-node rather than a soft dip. Without it the audit function is always restoring and the system has a single basin — which is what the first build produced, and why the bifurcation panel initially showed a smooth curve.

Governance quality

G* = 0.40 + 0.15·tanh(mean ethics) + 0.20·ι + 0.13·transparency
        + 0.09·budget + 0.06·reporter − 1.15·cap_frac^0.72 − 0.10·shock
G  = 0.84·G + 0.16·G* + 0.010·N(0,1)                    institutional inertia

05.3 Limitations

  • Nothing is calibrated. All quantities are properties of chosen equations. A detection delay of 1.8 years is not an estimate of anything.
  • The functional forms are the finding. Channel correlation dominates because it was written as 1 + (k−1)(1−ρ). That form is defensible, but it is an assumption, and the ranking inherits it.
  • The screen measures main effects. Standardised regression coefficients are linear-additive. Term length and the hard duration cap both rank low while being strongly interaction-dependent — they matter enormously in specific corners and barely on average. A low β means "not a reliable lever across the whole space", not "irrelevant".
  • Officials are a fixed pool of 64 with no factions, no parties and no constituency, so coalition dynamics are absent.
  • Harm is a single undifferentiated hazard. No types, no severities, no perpetrator model beyond the aggregate concealment term.
