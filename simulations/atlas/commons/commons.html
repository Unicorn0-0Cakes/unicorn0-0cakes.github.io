The Commons is a model, not an experiment. It dramatizes public-goods dilemmas, conditional cooperation, reputation and punishment dynamics described in cooperation research (Ostrom, Fehr & Gächter, Axelrod). Numbers are illustrative of mechanisms, not measurements of any real population. Part of the Simulations collection.
