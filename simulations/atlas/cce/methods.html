01 The source

The Cognitive Civilization Experiment is not a reconstruction of anything. It is an agent-based research instrument comparing three systems for allocating work, housing, assistance, representation and social responsibility in a fictional, fully simulated society of 100,000 citizens over 500 years.

Scientific disclaimer

This is a simulation. No human subjects, no real-world enforcement, no policy implementation. No parameter is calibrated to empirical data. Results are reproducible findings conditional on the model's assumptions and parameter ranges — never indisputable, and never claims about real people.

The three societies

Government, population cap, fertility policy, measurement rules, mortality model, safeguarding regime and the external event history are identical across arms. Runs sharing a seed share an identical baseline population and an identical shock history, so differences are attributable to the allocation rule and to nothing else.

What the page actually is

cce.html is a results explorer, not a live simulation. It embeds real output from completed runs at build time, with no second implementation of the model. The engine is Python and runs offline; regenerate the page from stored output with python3 analysis/export_web_data.py --pilot batches/rehearsal-30.

02 Rules and equations

The full specification is the ODD protocol in docs/ODD_PROTOCOL.md. The models that matter for reading the results:

Cognition — two measurements, not one number

The model is built so that no single number is allowed to explain a person. There is a latent capability across eleven dimensions, and separately an observed test score produced by an observation model with error, illness and stress. Society A allocates on the observed score; the gap between latent and observed is where much of A's behaviour comes from. emergent

The causal spine

latent capability (11 dims, partly hidden) ─┐ environment (accessibility, support, luck) ─┴─▶ observed test score ─▶ health literacy ──▶ mortality hazard ─▶ adaptive functioning ─▶ occupational fit └─▶ official class · support need · performance └─▶ outcomes: lifespan, independence

Health literacy is the only route to mortality, and the hazard it produces is attenuable to near zero. Support is allocated on need, never on score, in every arm.

Other registered models

  • Health and mortality — pathways, hazards, a Sullivan life table.
  • Population — cap, permits, waiting, inheritance.
  • Governance — presidency, assembly, accountability.
  • Safeguarding — welfare checks, detection, duration cap.
  • Support — adaptive functioning, needs, support levels, housing.

Each has its own document under docs/. Sentinel exists because two outputs of the governance and safeguarding models turned out to be structurally severed from the treatment — see Sentinel's methods.

03 Parameters

92 registered parameters, parameter set 2d6343f41b392d36, generated from code into docs/PARAMETER_REGISTER.md rather than maintained by hand. Every stored column is defined in docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md, and completeness is enforced by analysis/check_data_dictionary.py.

Experimental design

  • Matched-seed triads. One seed produces A, B and C runs sharing a baseline population and shock history.
  • Preregistered effect sizes. Smallest effect sizes of interest are declared in docs/HYPOTHESES.md alongside H1–H10, each with a mechanism, a measure and a falsifier.
  • Determinism contract. Separate random streams, replay, and provenance — docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md.

04 What is simplified

Every open decision and the default chosen for it is recorded in docs/ASSUMPTION_REGISTER.md. The structural simplifications that most affect how the results should be read:

  • One society per run, with no outside. No trade, no migration between arms, no external labour market.
  • Allocation is the only treatment. Everything else is held identical, which is what makes the contrast clean and also what makes it narrow.
  • Assistance is allocated on assessed need in all three arms. The arms differ in how work, housing and office are allocated, not in whether help is available.
  • Eleven competency dimensions are the whole of a person's capability. They are a modelling device, not a taxonomy anyone should defend.
  • Mortality has one route. Health literacy is the only pathway, which is a strong and deliberate constraint.
  • Governance is thin. Presidency, assembly and accountability, with no parties, no press and no courts — which is precisely why two of its outputs carried no signal.

05 What this cannot establish

Read this before quoting any number from the page

No production campaign has been run and no scientific findings exist. The only quantitative results in the repository are benchmark measurements, test outcomes, and exploratory-tagged engineering runs. A working batch runner does not make an experiment scientifically ready.

  • It cannot say anything about real people, real cognitive ability, or real policy. It is a fictional society, and docs/ETHICS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md exists to keep that clear.
  • It cannot report an effect size you should believe. The preregistration is a draft, not frozen, and the runs behind the explorer are tagged exploratory.
  • It cannot be calibrated by construction — no parameter is fitted to data, so the model has no empirical anchor to be right or wrong against.
  • It cannot support conclusions about detection delay or governance quality. Those two outputs were shown to be structurally severed from the treatment; that finding is what Sentinel was built to pursue.
  • It cannot generalise beyond the sampled parameter ranges, and the sensitivity work that would bound that is listed as outstanding.

What validation is still missing

  • Pattern validation against any external reference.
  • Golden regression digests.
  • A frozen preregistration — docs/PREREGISTRATION_DRAFT.md is a draft.
  • The pilot blockers listed in docs/PILOT_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md §6.

Twenty-five design documents cover the research question, hypotheses, ODD protocol, experimental design, statistical analysis plan, validation plan, risk register and reproducibility contract. That apparatus is the strongest on this site — and it is also what makes the honest status "no findings yet" rather than a result.
