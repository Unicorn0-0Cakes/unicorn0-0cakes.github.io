The Cognitive Civilization Experiment

Three simulated societies of 100,000 people run for 500 years under identical disasters, identical government and identical medicine. The only thing that differs is how they decide who does what — and whether the world is built to be understood.

This is a simulation, not a study of people. No human subjects, no policy, no empirical calibration — all 92 parameters are marked not yet calibrated. Every number here is a property of a computer model, reproducible only under its stated assumptions. Nothing on this page is evidence about the abilities, needs or worth of any real person or group, and none of the three societies is endorsed.

The question

If a civilization has to decide who gets which job, which home and which kind of help, does it matter whether it sorts people by an IQ score, by what they can demonstrably do, or by removing the obstacles that make ordinary life hard in the first place?

What is held identical

Two societies running the same seed get the same starting population, citizen for citizen, and the same 500-year history of disasters, epidemics, discoveries and scandals — verified byte-for-byte. If their outcomes differ, the allocation rule is why.

How cognition is allowed to matter

IQ is never permitted to act as a universal cause. It reaches an outcome only through a named, inspectable pathway, and every pathway can be weakened by building an environment that is easier to navigate. Set accessibility to maximum and the health gradient nearly vanishes — that is a mechanism in the code, not an assumed result.

Controlled and differing variables

Government structure, population cap, fertility policy, measurement rules, mortality model, healthcare access and the safeguarding regime are identical across all three arms, so that only the allocation system varies.

Held constant across all three

What support means here

Assistance follows assessed need in every arm — Society A allocates jobs, housing and office by score, never assistance. A high-scoring citizen with a disability, executive-function limitation or illness receives substantial support; a low-scoring citizen in a familiar, accessible environment may need almost none. Both directions are enforced invariants, checked every run.

Nine support levels

  1. Independent
  2. Light support
  3. Scheduled assistance
  4. Daily assistance
  5. Continuous remote monitoring
  6. Supported housing
  7. Live-in assistance
  8. Medical residential support
  9. Secure protective care

More supervision is never treated as better: unnecessary supervision costs adaptive functioning every year it continues, and restrictive housing raises measured vulnerability to abuse.

Two failure modes, both measured

Under-support — assessed need exceeds assigned support. Raises mortality hazard and vulnerability.

Over-support — supervision exceeds need. Costs independence at a fixed rate per year.

The needs assessment is deliberately noisy. That noise is what produces both failure modes; setting it to zero is a sensitivity condition, not the default.

Final state

Cognitive measurement drift

Two measurements are kept and never conflated. Relative civic IQ is re-normalised after every 5-year assessment, so it sits at 100 by construction forever. Absolute capability is referenced to the fixed Year-0 scale and is free to drift. Reporting only the first would hide any real change in the population.

Matched-seed treatment estimates

Why the flags matter

With enough runs, almost any non-zero difference becomes statistically unambiguous. That is a fact about sample size, not importance. Contrasts whose confidence interval excludes zero while the estimate sits below the preregistered smallest effect size of interest are flagged precise but below SESOI — the honest label for a difference you can measure but should not care about.

Between-seed variability

The noise floor. If this is far below the smallest effect worth caring about, then more runs buy precision nobody needs, and the real uncertainty lives in the parameters instead.

Does stochastic history move the outcomes?

Each run's outcomes correlated with its own exposure to disasters. If these were all near zero and between-seed variance were near zero, a thousand-run campaign would be a very precise measurement of a model that barely reacts to its own history.

Government

The same constitution runs in all three arms, so politics is not a hidden treatment. The highest official score becomes president — but eligibility is not competence. Leadership ability and ethics are drawn with a deliberately weak link to the score (0.20 for systems planning, 0.00 for ethics), because the model must be able to produce a bad high-scoring leader.

Presidential score over five centuries

How each presidency was decided

Scores are capped at a reportable 150 while latent capability is retained separately, so ties at the ceiling are common. Only tied citizens vote, and only among themselves; a tied vote is broken deterministically by lowest citizen id so the run can be replayed exactly.

Representation

Every populated IQ band holds at least one seat at all times — not merely on election day. Deaths, removals and successions trigger by-elections, and a run fails verification if any populated band is ever unrepresented.

Accountability

No band receives immunity. A president found corrupt is removed and charged exactly as a representative is.

Methodology

Summarised from the ODD protocol. Full specification lives in the repository's docs/ directory — 20 documents covering the research question, hypotheses, every sub-model, the statistical analysis plan and the preregistration draft.

The ten constructs that stay separate

Cognitive ability · adaptive functioning · learned competence · educational attainment · physical and mental health · executive functioning · social ability · occupational skill · environmental support · random opportunity and adversity. Each is stored and updated independently, so no single variable can stand in for a person.

Observed score is not latent capability 
observed = battery composite
         − sensory penalty  × (1 − 0.9 · accessibility)
         − acute illness    × (1 − 0.5 · accessibility)
         − stress           × (1 − 0.5 · accessibility)
         + practice effect
         + motivation ~ N(0, 2)
         + measurement error ~ N(0, 15·√(1 − reliability))

At the default reliability of 0.92 the measurement error is about 4.2 points. Every official score is stored with its standard error, and a citizen scoring 89 is not treated as categorically different from one scoring 90. Note the direction of the accessibility terms: an inaccessible testing environment lowers the measured score of an impaired citizen without lowering their capability, so part of any classification difference is measurement artefact by construction — and is reported as such.

The official battery deliberately does not measure everything

Six psychometric dimensions enter the official score. Practical judgment, social understanding, emotional regulation and consistency do not — yet jobs require them. That gap is the modelled source of Society A's classification error, rather than an assertion that "IQ isn't everything".

Testing schedule, and no appeals

Assessment is civilization-wide every 5 years on the anniversary, not on birthdays. Civic classification begins at age 20, so a citizen born just after a cycle can be 24 and still unclassified — a pattern the verification checks for explicitly. There is no code path from a citizen to a test request: no appeals, no elective retesting. Only a documented medical event can trigger an emergency assessment.

Population is capped structurally

The state is a fixed array of exactly 100,000 slots. A citizen holds one for life; death returns it to a free stack and creates a population opening. A birth needs a free slot, so the cap cannot be exceeded by any code path, and no living citizen can be silently deleted. Every denied or postponed birth is logged.

Abuse is not set to zero

A model where harm cannot occur cannot measure whether safeguarding works. Severe-abuse situations are initiated stochastically, weighted by dependence, isolation, restrictive housing and unmet need. What the civilization controls is duration: nothing may persist undetected beyond the configured maximum, and the cost of that regime — false positives, expense, intrusion — is measured alongside its benefit. Everything is abstract: states, counts and delays only.

Known limitations
  • No spatial structure, no markets or prices, no household objects, no migration.
  • Epidemics are exogenous shocks, not transmission processes — any pandemic-related reading carries this caveat.
  • No assortative mating, which matters materially for the fertility-policy hypothesis.
  • Governance acts through a single quality index; no legislation or coalitions yet.
  • Perpetrators are not strategic agents, so detection performance is likely optimistic.
  • 500 years at fixed parameters implies an implausibly static institutional world.

Status

Milestone 0 (specification) and the reference kernel are complete; the matched-seed batch runner has passed five validation gates. No production campaign has been run and no scientific findings exist.

Outstanding before any preregistered campaign: empirical calibration, preregistration freeze, pattern-oriented validation, and global sensitivity analysis — parameter uncertainty is the dominant source and is currently unmeasured. Repeated runs address stochastic uncertainty only.
