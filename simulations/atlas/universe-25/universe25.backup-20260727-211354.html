Based on John B. Calhoun's Universe 25 experiment (NIMH, 1968–1972), where a colony with unlimited resources but fixed space grew, socially fractured, and died out — the "behavioral sink." The predator layer is a modern extension: with hunters present, predation can hold density below the breakdown threshold and stave off collapse — or tip the whole system into extinction. A simplified agent model for exploration, not a scientific reproduction.
