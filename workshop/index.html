The Workshop

Small things. A day, a weekend, an afternoon spent finding out whether an idea was any good. They are here because interesting small work should not have to pretend to be large work to be shown.

01 Entries

Everything in this drawer is labelled Workshop, which means: worth showing, not offered as a major piece. None of it has been polished for display, and several entries are unfinished in ways the description says out loud.

02 What is not in here

A workshop stops being useful the moment it becomes a junk drawer. Public repositories that are forks, mirrors, planning documents or course exercises are not listed on this site at all — they are recorded in the project registry as hidden, so the decision is written down rather than forgotten. Being public on GitHub and belonging on a portfolio are two different things.
