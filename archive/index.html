01 Why these are archived rather than deleted

Three reasons. The work itself is real and has been brought forward into Design, re-encoded and without the marketing wrapper. The pages also carry engagement percentages, time-on-market figures and client testimonials that cannot be substantiated, and deleting them quietly would be a worse answer than leaving them somewhere they can be seen for what they are. And nothing on a portfolio should vanish without a trace — the full history is in this repository's git log regardless.

These pages are marked noindex, are not linked from the site navigation, and carry a banner identifying them as superseded. They are kept for reference, not for reading.
